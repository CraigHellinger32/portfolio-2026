Food waste container and green bin

When is my next bin collection?

What can I use the green bin or food waste container for?

You can put the following items in your green bin or food waste container.

  • Garden waste
  • Food waste

You can put pet bedding from domestic pets, such as rabbits, guinea pigs and hamsters for example in your green bin. The following types are allowed: Hay, sawdust, straw, wood shavings and chipped wood.

Do not put the following items in the green bin:

  • Soil / compost
  • Cat litter or dog waste should go in the grey bin

How can I get more compostable liners?

When you are running low on liners, please tie an empty liner around the handle of your green bin or food waste container on collection day and the crew will leave a new roll of liners. You can also go to the One Stop Shop at Bolton Town Hall or the UCAN Centres for replacement rolls of liners.

Can I have more than one green bin?

It has always been policy to issue one green bin per household since they were rolled out in 2003/04.

We encourage all residents to recycle as much of their household waste as possible, but the green bin waste service was introduced to prevent food and garden waste being disposed of in the grey bins. Unlike the recycling from your beige and burgundy bins which generates an income, food and garden waste costs the council to process each tonne collected. It is significantly cheaper than the waste going in the grey bins, but more expensive then residents composting at home.

We would encourage residents with large gardens to compost at home, as this is one of the most ecologically friendly ways to deal with garden waste. Further information about subsidised compost bins is available at www.getcomposting.
